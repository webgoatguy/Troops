How to move

Points mode: stones never die and can't move into walls or other stones. Blue's points are in the top row and Red's in the bottom row. Land a stone on one of your points to pick it up (the stone turns lighter with a gold dot), then bring it home to score: Blue's home is the bottom 5 rows, Red's the top 5. Moving a blob drags everything stuck to it, of either color. Any move that puts a blue stone on a red point, or a red stone on a blue point, is illegal.

  • Click a stone: its king moves appear.
  • Click the same stone again: its whole blob is selected, and that stone gets a ring. A blob can slide to any spot where its new position overlaps or edge-touches its old one.
  • In blob mode, the shaded squares show everywhere the ringed stone can go. Hover to preview the whole blob, then click to move it. You can also drag any stone of the blob to where you want that stone to end up.
  • Or nudge the preview with ←↑→↓ and press Enter.
  • Click the ringed stone again (or Esc) to deselect.
  • Shift+click one of your own adjacent stones to deliberately move into it (you lose the moving stone).
  • Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.
  • Placing: press an "Add … stone" button, then click or drag across squares. Clicking a stone of that color removes it. Press the button again or Esc when done.

Blue scores off the top edge, Red off the bottom edge. Stones pushed off the left/right edges (or off the opponent's scoring edge) just disappear.

In blob mode, stones that will be dragged along get a dashed outline. A stone carries at most one point at a time. A dark × marks a point square a stone isn't allowed onto.
